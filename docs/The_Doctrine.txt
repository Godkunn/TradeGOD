LAYER 1 — ALPHA RULES  (When is the environment worth scanning?)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE A1: Trend Vector (SMA 50 vs SMA 200 on 1h)
  BULL  → Price > SMA50 > SMA200   → Only BUY signals allowed
  BEAR  → Price < SMA50 < SMA200   → Only SELL signals allowed
  VOID  → Mixed / Crossing         → NO TRADE. Bot sleeps.

RULE A2: ADX Strength
  ADX > 25  → Trending market  → Bot may proceed to Beta scan
  ADX < 20  → Ranging market   → Bot sleeps. Ranging kills bots.

RULE A3: Structural Proximity
  Bot only cares when price is within 1.2% of:
    - Recent 50-bar HIGH  (Resistance zone)
    - Recent 50-bar LOW   (Support zone)
  If price is floating in no-man's land → Bot sleeps.

COMBINED ALPHA GATE:
  All 3 must pass → Zone Active → Pass to Beta
  Any 1 fails     → Zone Void  → Sleep 5 minutes

LAYER 2 — BETA RULES  (Is there an exact trigger right now?)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

PATTERN HIERARCHY (scored out of 100)

  +25 pts  REJECTION WICK
           Wick ≥ 62% of total candle range.
           Body is small (exhaustion, not momentum).
           Bullish: Long lower wick at support.
           Bearish: Long upper wick at resistance.

  +25 pts  ENGULFING CANDLE
           Current body completely swallows previous body.
           Direction must reverse.
           Bullish engulf at support → BUY.
           Bearish engulf at resistance → SELL.

  +20 pts  FALSE BREAKOUT TRAP
           Price wick broke through key level but closed back inside.
           This traps retail traders on the wrong side.
           One of the highest-probability setups in existence.

  +15 pts  RSI ALIGNMENT
           RSI < 35 (oversold) for BUY signals.
           RSI > 65 (overbought) for SELL signals.
           Confirmation, not primary signal.

  +15 pts  VOLUME SPIKE
           Volume ≥ 1.5x the 20-bar average.
           Without volume, a pattern is noise.

BETA FIRE THRESHOLD: confidence ≥ 40 pts
  Below 40 → stalk (check again in 2 min)
  40–59    → valid signal, proceed to Risk check
  60–79    → strong signal
  80–100   → rare, highest probability setup

LAYER 3 — RISK OVERLORD RULES  (Absolute & Non-Negotiable)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

RULE R1: 2% Hard Cap
  Maximum risk per trade = 2% of current account balance.
  Position size is calculated FROM stop-loss distance.
  Not from arbitrary lot sizes.

RULE R2: Minimum R:R = 2.0
  Take-Profit must be at minimum 2x the Stop-Loss distance.
  If market structure doesn't allow it → NO TRADE.

RULE R3: Binance $5 Minimum
  If calculated position notional < $5.50 → NO TRADE.
  This is a hard exchange limit.

RULE R4: Daily Circuit Breaker
  If total daily loss ≥ 3% of capital → SHUTDOWN for the day.
  Return next UTC day. No exceptions. No revenge trading.

RULE R5: 15-Minute Post-Trade Cooldown
  After ANY executed trade → mandatory 15-min silence.
  The market will still be there. Your capital won't be
  if you panic-trade after a loss.

FIXED LEVELS (config/settings.py):
  Stop Loss     : 1.2% from entry
  Take Profit   : 2.8% from entry
  R:R Ratio     : ~2.3x
